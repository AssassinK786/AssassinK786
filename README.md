### 🧑‍💻 About Me

I work at the intersection of **digital hardware, FPGA toolchains, ASIC design, embedded systems, and open-source EDA**.

I'm particularly interested in understanding the complete hardware stack — from **RTL and synthesis to placement, routing, bitstreams, and physical design**.

* 🔭 Working on **FPGA toolchains, RTL design, and open-source EDA**
* 🧩 Exploring **FPGA architecture, device databases, synthesis, place & route, and bitstream generation**
* 🔬 Building and experimenting with **RTL → GDS flows using open-source tools**
* ⚡ Developing **FPGA + embedded hardware/software systems**
* 🤖 Exploring **AI/LLMs, reinforcement learning, and agentic workflows for hardware engineering**
* 🌱 Currently deepening my knowledge of **digital design, computer architecture, RISC-V, FPGA architecture, and physical design**
* 🤝 Interested in collaborating on **FPGA, ASIC, RTL, EDA, RISC-V, Embedded, and AI × Hardware projects**
